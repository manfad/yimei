Palm oil mills produce a large volume of wastewater, known as palm oil mill effluent (POME), and treating it properly is essential for any mill. **Keningau Palm Oil Mill (KPOM)** ([kpom.my](https://kpom.my)), our sister company in Keningau, Sabah, is no exception, and it is where Yimei's treatment equipment is now hard at work.

These photos and videos, taken on **9 October 2026**, show the treatment plant at KPOM from the air and up close.

![](/images/events/kpom-and-yimei-team-up-on-mill-wastewater-treatment/photo-1.jpg)

## The treatment plant from the air

The plant sits beside the mill, surrounded by oil palms. Wastewater flows through a series of concrete treatment basins, with our blue treatment units housed under the covered shed in front.

![](/images/events/kpom-and-yimei-team-up-on-mill-wastewater-treatment/photo-2.jpg)

## Our equipment up close

Under the shed, chemical dosing tanks feed the treatment units, and skimmers on our flotation tanks continuously remove the sludge that collects on the surface, separating solids from the water.

## Better together

For KPOM, this means the mill's wastewater is treated on site with equipment built for the job. For Yimei, KPOM is a working example of what our equipment can do at a palm oil mill.
